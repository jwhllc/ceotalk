The next chapter

Depth and stability.

Lucas and I built BRYC into what it is together. I led major parts of the work, and we shaped the culture, systems and branding behind our Best Place to Work recognition — seven years on Baton Rouge's list, and #2 among nonprofits nationally in 2026. Stepping into this role, the job isn't to expand fast; it's to keep what we built steady and strong. We reach more students by going deeper into what already works, not by spreading thin. I'm open to wider growth when it's the right kind; for now, the work is stability and depth.

How we get there

The work, in three parts.

Programs

Go deeper, not wider.

The mission stays the work — alumni support, advising, senior counseling, ACT, self-regulated learning and youth participatory action research, for students whose communities have been underserved and any student with a real need for BRYC. The next chapter deepens our mission: career-oriented experiential learning, a codified model and data that proves what's working.

Sustainability

Fund the mission so it lasts.

One person shouldn't carry fundraising. We build it two ways.

Raise it

Go after industry dollars, large grants and government money, and build a real development team — so fundraising isn't one person's job.

Earn it

We're content experts who lead young people well — and people will pay to learn from us. We have a platform; this is about capitalizing on it.

Conferences we host Trainings & speakers we convene ACT bootcamps, open to the public Content as thought leaders Studio & campus space Tuition from over-income families
People

Build so it doesn't depend on any one person.

A leadership structure that outlasts any individual — including me. We invest in the next generation of leaders, keep the team culture a place people grow into and carry everyone through the transition with stability.

Year one

Where I start.

First, a strong and stable start to the program year — before any big moves. Then the near-term work:

  • Bring on a strong development lead — and clarify how roles shift around it.
  • Map the remaining team gaps and fill them deliberately.
  • Build communications and storytelling that match the best in the business.
  • Decide the future of BRYC Airline by spring 2027 — after giving the space every shot, and deepening the inroads we've already made into Ascension.
  • Pursue state and federal funding.
  • Bring more voices to the table — a leadership council.

How I lead

Efficiency is a love language for me. I clear the way so the team can do their best work, and I see ideas through — that's how we open pathways for our Fellows and alumni. I take real swings, too: I stood up BRYC Iberville — a satellite campus — in under three months with committed partners, ran it start to finish, and we just secured funding for these Fellows for a fourth year. That expansion is what grew us into our Airline campus. I won't wait for perfect to pursue something worth doing.

For a long time, I didn't want this role. Being number two let me serve my mission, gave me room to do work I love here and outside of BRYC, and let me lead beside my best friend. Then Lucas asked me, "Why not you?" — and it landed differently. I got curious. I prayed on it, sought counsel, and it still met my mission, my values and my goals. So here I am, ready to pick up the mantle.

Reaching them. And building something strong enough to keep reaching them.
