The work

At BRYC since 2017 — director of persistence, director of community, and now chief of staff. Outside it, I keep my hand in work I find meaningful: communications consultant and campaign manager for the East Baton Rouge District Attorney, adjunct at LSU's Manship School and Ogden Honors College, emcee for LSU Athletics and consulting.

I'm a contributing author to the forthcoming "Counseling Athletes: Mental Health Across the Lifespan," with a chapter on how athletes can use social media well — an extension of my graduate work in applied positive psychology at the University of Pennsylvania.
